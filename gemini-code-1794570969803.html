Academic Focus & Candidate Profile

Designed for graduates and working professionals transitioning into formal project, program, or portfolio leadership.

Who Should Apply

  • Project & Product Coordinators: Professionals formalizing their practice toward Project Manager, Scrum Master, or PMO roles.
  • EU Funds & Public Sector Specialists: Practitioners managing non-reimbursable grants, structural instruments, and public investments.
  • Founders & Operational Leads: Entrepreneurs structuring product delivery, budgeting, and risk governance.
  • Consultants & Business Analysts: Specialists seeking alignment with international PMI standards.

Key Program Characteristics

  • PMI GAC International Accreditation: The only program in Romania accredited by the PMI Global Accreditation Center (reconfirmed 2026–2032).
  • Balanced Methodology: Equal emphasis on predictive planning (WBS, Critical Path, Earned Value) and adaptive frameworks (Scrum, Kanban).
  • Practitioner Faculty: Courses taught by PMP-certified consultants, EU grant evaluators, and academic researchers.
  • Flexible Attendance: Full-time English track alongside part-time and low-frequency (IFR) tracks in Romanian.
