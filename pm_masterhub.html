Program Overview

Who This Master's Program Is For & Key Benefits

Dedicated to training professionals capable of coordinating complex projects in modern organizations across the public, private, and non-governmental sectors.

Who Should Apply & Why It Matters

As organizations increasingly develop their operations through projects, project management has become a strategic competency in the labor market. This program is designed for:

  • Project Team Members: Professionals pursuing a formal career as a Project Manager, Program Director, or Planner.
  • Public & EU Program Specialists: Experts managing grant funds, European programs, and public investments.
  • Entrepreneurs & Business Leaders: Founders intending to attract European funding and scale operations systematically.
  • Consultants & Trainers: Practitioners seeking alignment with international PMI standards and professional certifications.

Program Curriculum & Course Outlines

Review all 16 courses across Year 1 and Year 2, including credits, duration, prerequisites, and key learning outcomes.

Year 18 ECTS14 Weeks

Project Management I

Foundational predictive project management processes: initiating, scope definition, WBS, scheduling, and budgeting.

Instructor: Prof. Dr. Simona Bonghez, PMP

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Develop a complete project management plan and Work Breakdown Structure aligned with PMBOK standards.

Year 17 ECTS14 Weeks

Entrepreneurship and Innovation

Cultivating entrepreneurial thinking and designing innovative products and services through project-based initiatives.

Instructor: Dr. Lavinia Iancu

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Formulate business models, evaluate market feasibility, and pitch innovative project ventures.

Year 18 ECTS14 Weeks

Leadership and Management of the Project Team

Managing group dynamics, team motivation, conflict resolution, and leadership styles across project life cycles.

Instructor: Lect. Univ. Dr. Carmen Novac

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Guide teams through Forming, Storming, Norming, and Performing stages with empathetic leadership.

Year 18 ECTS14 Weeks

European Funding

Attracting, writing, and implementing grant-funded projects financed through European Union programs.

Instructor: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Elena Negrea-Busuioc

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Draft compliant EU funding proposals, logical matrices, and public grant budgets.

Year 18 ECTS14 Weeks

Project Management II

Advanced execution, earned value management (EVM), quality control, procurement, and project closure.

Instructor: Prof. Dr. Simona Bonghez, PMP

Prerequisites: Project Management I

Key Learning Outcomes: Monitor project baselines using Earned Value metrics and execute formal contract closures.

Year 17 ECTS14 Weeks

Presentation Skills for Project Managers

Executive communication, stakeholder pitching, negotiation, and persuasive reporting for project leaders.

Instructor: Cda Dr. Georgiana Cristea

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Deliver clear stakeholder demos, status briefings, and executive steering presentations.

Year 17 ECTS14 Weeks

Research Methods in Communication Sciences

Quantitative and qualitative research methodologies for organizational analysis and applied project studies.

Instructors: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Valeriu Frunzaru & Prof. Dr. Loredana Ivan

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Design surveys, interview guides, and empirical data analyses for project research.

Year 17 ECTS14 Weeks

Strategic Management

Aligning project portfolios with organizational strategy, competitive advantage, and long-term governance.

Instructor: Cda Dr. Cristina Racoceanu

Prerequisites: None

Key Learning Outcomes: Evaluate strategic alignment of project portfolios and organizational value delivery.

Year 28 ECTS14 Weeks

Agile Project Management Methodologies

Principles and practices of Agile, Scrum, Kanban, and Extreme Programming (XP) for dynamic environments.

Instructors: Prof. Dr. Simona Bonghez, PMP / C.d.a. Adina Grigoroiu

Prerequisites: Project Management I & II

Key Learning Outcomes: Compare predictive and adaptive lifecycles and deliver a product prototype using Scrum and Trello.

Year 27 ECTS14 Weeks

Applications for Project Management

Digital software tools for project scheduling, resource allocation, tracking, and collaborative workflows.

Instructor: Prof. Mirela Pirvan, PhD

Prerequisites: Project Management I

Key Learning Outcomes: Configure project schedules, resource calendars, and digital tracking boards.

Year 28 ECTS14 Weeks

Risk Management

Qualitative and quantitative risk analysis, mitigation strategies, and managing project uncertainty.

Instructor: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Sergiu Stan

Prerequisites: Project Management I

Key Learning Outcomes: Utilize risk management instruments for identifying, analyzing, and mitigating project risks.

Year 27 ECTS14 Weeks

Preparation and Implementation of Research Projects

Designing, funding, and coordinating national and international R&D and academic research projects.

Instructor: Prof. Dr. Adrian Curaj

Prerequisites: Research Methods

Key Learning Outcomes: Structure multi-partner research consortia, work packages, and deliverable milestones.

Year 27 ECTS14 Weeks

Project Evaluation and Audit

Compliance verification, quality auditing, benefit realization, and post-implementation project evaluation.

Instructor: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Sergiu Stan

Prerequisites: Project Management II

Key Learning Outcomes: Conduct formal project audits and evaluate compliance against contractual objectives.

Year 27 ECTS14 Weeks

Monitoring and Performance Indicators

Designing KPIs, control charts, dashboards, and performance reporting systems for projects.

Instructor: Dr. Irina Lonean

Prerequisites: Project Management I

Key Learning Outcomes: Define measurable KPIs and track project health across predictive and adaptive workflows.

Year 28 ECTS14 Weeks

Specialized Practice (Internship)

Supervised practical placement in partner companies, public institutions, or project management offices.

Instructor: Dr. Marina Enache

Prerequisites: Year 1 Completed

Key Learning Outcomes: Apply project management tools in a real organizational environment and compile a practice portfolio.

Year 28 ECTS14 Weeks

Methods and Techniques for Developing the Graduation Thesis

Structured academic guidance for researching, writing, and defending the Master's dissertation.

Instructor: Cda Dr. Iulia Calin

Prerequisites: Research Methods

Key Learning Outcomes: Complete a rigorous, original Master's dissertation addressing an applied PM topic.
