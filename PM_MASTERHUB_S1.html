Program Overview

Whether you just finished your Bachelor's degree or already work in a team, this program helps you build the practical skills to organize projects, lead people, and grow your career in business, tech, or European-funded initiatives.

Who this program is for

  • Recent Graduates: Looking for a practical master's degree that opens doors in any industry.
  • Junior Team Members: Ready to step up from executing tasks to coordinating projects and teams.
  • EU Funds & Public Sector Beginners: Interested in writing and managing European-funded projects.
  • Future Entrepreneurs: Wanting to turn business ideas into well-structured, successful projects.

Key Benefits

  • Internationally recognized diploma: The only master's in Romania accredited by PMI (Global Accreditation Center).
  • Learn by doing: Practical workshops, real case studies, and team simulations instead of dry theory.
  • Teachers who work in the field: Learn directly from active project managers and industry specialists.
  • Schedule built around your life: Choose between regular classes (IF) or weekend-friendly format (IFR).
